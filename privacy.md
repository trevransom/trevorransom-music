Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 10, 2026
1. Introduction
This privacy policy describes how personal information is processed in
connection with the private software applications operated by Trevor
Ransom.

These applications include:
- Home Calendar MCP
- Personal Finance MCP
The applications are private, non-commercial projects developed
exclusively for personal use.
2. Information Processed
Depending on which application is used, information processed may
include:
Home Calendar MCP
- Calendar events and schedules.
- Event descriptions and associated metadata.
- Calendar account identifiers.
Personal Finance MCP
- Bank account information.
- Account balances.
- Transaction descriptions, dates and amounts.
- Merchant information.
- Spending categories and budgets.
- Investment and asset information entered into the application.
- Financial summaries and calculations.
3. Sources of Information
Information may be obtained from services explicitly connected to the
applications, including:
- Google Calendar.
- S-Pankki.
- Open Banking providers such as Enable Banking.
- Manually uploaded financial statements or transaction exports.
- Other financial information entered by the operator.
Bank account access requires explicit authorisation through the relevant
banking provider.
4. How Information Is Used
Information is processed for personal purposes, including:
- Managing personal calendars and schedules.
- Tracking income and expenses.
- Categorising financial transactions.
- Monitoring account balances and savings.
- Calculating net worth and financial trends.
- Managing budgets and financial goals.
- Generating personal financial summaries and insights.
Information is not collected for advertising, profiling other
individuals or commercial resale.
5. AI Services
The applications may integrate with AI services such as ChatGPT and
Claude.

When the operator uses these integrations, relevant information may be
transmitted to the selected AI provider to answer questions or perform
requested analysis.

The information shared depends on the requested operation and the data
made available to the AI integration.

AI providers process information under their own privacy policies and
applicable service terms.
6. Data Storage and Security
The applications are designed to operate on privately controlled
infrastructure.

Security measures may include:
- Private network access.
- Authentication and access controls.
- Encrypted connections.
- Restricted access to application credentials.
- Database backups and security controls.
Banking credentials and private API keys should not be shared through
conversational interfaces.
7. Third-Party Providers
The applications may rely on third-party services to provide
functionality.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy
policies and contractual obligations.

Relevant providers may include:
- S-Pankki.
- Enable Banking.
- Google.
- OpenAI.
- Anthropic.
Their respective privacy policies and data processing practices apply to
information they process.
8. Data Retention
Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the intended
personal functionality.

Financial transaction history may be retained to support historical
spending analysis, financial reporting and reconciliation.

Information may be deleted when it is no longer needed.

Retention periods may also depend on the policies of connected
third-party providers.
9. Data Sharing
The applications are not intended to provide financial or personal
information to other users or commercial customers.

Information may be transmitted to connected service providers when
necessary to perform an explicitly requested operation.

Information is not sold or shared for advertising purposes.
10. Data Protection and Individual Rights
Questions concerning personal information, access, correction or
deletion can be directed to the operator.

Where applicable, rights under the General Data Protection Regulation
(GDPR) and other relevant data protection legislation apply.
11. Changes to This Policy
This policy may be updated as the applications evolve, new integrations
are introduced or data processing practices change.

The latest version will be published at:

https://trevorransom.com/privacy
