Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Information Processed

My private applications may process information necessary for their functionality, including:

  • Calendar events and schedules.
  • Bank account information and balances.
  • Financial transactions and merchant information.
  • Budgets, spending categories and financial summaries.
  • Information manually entered by the operator.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used for personal purposes, including calendar management, expense tracking, budgeting, savings monitoring and financial analysis.

3. Third-Party Services

Applications may connect to Google Calendar, S-Pankki, Enable Banking, OpenAI (ChatGPT) and Anthropic (Claude).

When AI services are used, relevant information may be shared with the selected provider to perform requested analysis. These providers operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Data Storage and Security

The applications are designed to run on privately controlled infrastructure. Security measures include access controls, encrypted connections and restricted access to credentials.

5. Data Sharing

Personal information is not sold or used for advertising. Information may be shared with connected providers when necessary to perform requested functionality.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the applications' functionality and support historical analysis.

7. Contact
